Check the road before you leave.

Prometnik brings together everything you need before driving in Slovenia: closures, congestion, accidents and roadworks, road webcams, roadside weather, road-surface conditions and travel times between cities. Free, no account, no ads.

TRAFFIC EVENTS ON THE MAP
• Closures, congestion, accidents, roadworks and bora (burja) wind warnings from DARS / promet.si
• The affected road section is drawn on the map
• Satellite imagery with road labels, OpenStreetMap or Mapy.cz

WEBCAMS
• About 170 DARS, DRSC and ARSO road webcams
• 24-hour hourly history for weather-station cameras
• Stations with several cameras show every view

ROAD WEATHER AND SURFACE
• Temperature, humidity, wind, gusts, wind direction and rain from roadside stations
• 24-hour temperature, wind and rain charts
• Road temperature, freezing point, dew point, water film, salinity and ice warning

TRAVEL TIMES AND REST AREAS
• DARS travel times between cities with delays and a route mini-map
• Rest areas with parking occupancy and truck spaces

NEARBY AND ALERTS
• The Nearby tab shows events and webcams around you
• Notifications for the roads you watch
• Home-screen widget for a chosen station

PRIVACY
Location is used on the device only; notifications are local. No account, no tracking, no ads.

Prometnik is not an official DARS app. For official traffic information visit promet.si.
Data: DARS / promet.si, DRSI, ARSO, OpenStreetMap.
